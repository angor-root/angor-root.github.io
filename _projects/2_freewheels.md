**FreeWheels** is an intuitive-control wheelchair designed for athletes, developed at UTEC.

- Awarded **2nd place at IROS 2025** (Volting Cup), presented in China — one of the world's most important robotics conferences.
- Winner of the **Smart City Challenge**, awarded by Darat Latam and UTEC Ventures for its potential impact on smart urban environments.
