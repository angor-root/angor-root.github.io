Robotics learning resources

Courses, simulators, papers and open hardware I used to get into legged robotics. Free unless marked otherwise.

Audio versions of the papers: public NotebookLM notebook (Spanish).

Foundations

  • Modern Robotics Kevin Lynch & Frank Park · Northwestern · Book + course
    The cleanest introduction to screw theory, kinematics and dynamics. Free PDF, free lecture videos, and the notation the rest of the field uses.
  • Underactuated Robotics Russ Tedrake · MIT 6.832 · Course
    Why a walking robot is not a robot arm. Legged locomotion, trajectory optimization and LQR, taught from first principles with runnable notebooks.
  • Planning Algorithms Steven M. LaValle · free online · Book
    The reference for configuration spaces, sampling-based planning and motion under differential constraints. Read chapters 4 to 6 and come back for the rest.
  • Spinning Up in Deep RL OpenAI · Course
    The fastest honest path from "what is a policy gradient" to a working PPO implementation, which is the algorithm behind most legged RL controllers today.
  • Deep Reinforcement Learning, CS 285 Sergey Levine · UC Berkeley · Course
    Go here after Spinning Up, when you need model-based RL, offline RL and imitation learning to be more than words.
  • Robot Dynamics & Legged Robots Robotic Systems Lab · ETH Zürich · Lectures
    Floating-base dynamics, contact models and whole-body control, taught by the group that built ANYmal. Slides and exercises are public.

Simulators & code

  • MuJoCo Google DeepMind · open source · Simulator
    Fast, accurate contact dynamics with a readable XML model format. MJX runs thousands of environments on a GPU for RL. Best first simulator.
  • Isaac Lab NVIDIA · Simulator
    Where most published legged RL policies are trained now: massively parallel envs, domain randomization and sim-to-real tooling out of the box. Needs an NVIDIA GPU.
  • legged_gym + rsl_rl Robotic Systems Lab · ETH Zürich · Code
    The reference implementation of "train a quadruped to walk in minutes". Read it end to end, because the reward terms alone teach you more than a semester of theory.
  • Pinocchio Stack of Tasks · LAAS-CNRS · Library
    Rigid-body dynamics and its analytical derivatives, fast enough for control loops. What you reach for when you write your own whole-body controller.
  • Drake Toyota Research Institute · MIT · Toolbox
    Model-based design done seriously: multibody dynamics plus convex optimization and contact-implicit trajectory optimization in one toolbox.
  • ROS 2 documentation Open Robotics · Framework
    Not glamorous, unavoidable. Do the tutorials up to lifecycle nodes and tf2, because every robot you will touch in a lab speaks this.

Papers that changed legged robotics

  • Learning agile and dynamic motor skills for legged robots Hwangbo et al. · ETH Zürich · Science Robotics · 2019
    The paper that made sim-to-real RL credible on real hardware: learn the actuator, and the policy transfers. Start your reading here.
  • Learning quadrupedal locomotion over challenging terrain Lee et al. · ETH Zürich · Science Robotics · 2020
    Teacher and student training with proprioception only. ANYmal crossing mud, snow and rubble without ever seeing the ground.
  • Learning robust perceptive locomotion in the wild Miki et al. · ETH Zürich · Science Robotics · 2022
    How to use vision without trusting it: an attention-based belief state that falls back on proprioception when the elevation map lies.
  • Learning to Walk in Minutes Using Massively Parallel Deep RL Rudin et al. · ETH Zürich · CoRL · 2021
    The training recipe behind legged_gym, and the reason a student with one GPU can now reproduce this work.
  • RMA: Rapid Motor Adaptation Kumar, Fu, Pathak, Malik · CMU / Berkeley · RSS · 2021
    Adapting to a new payload or a slippery floor within a fraction of a second, by estimating the environment from recent state history.
  • Capturability-based analysis of legged locomotion Koolen, de Boer, Rebula, Goswami, Pratt · Foundational · 2012
    The capture-point theory behind push recovery, the classical, model-based counterweight to everything above. It is what my Obedience biped is built on.

Open hardware & staying current

  • Open Dynamic Robot Initiative MPI-IS & NYU · Solo 8 / Solo 12 · Open hardware
    A torque-controlled quadruped you can actually build: full BOM, 3D-printable parts, open firmware. The realistic entry point to real legged hardware.
  • moteus controllers mjbots · open source · Actuation
    Small, cheap field-oriented-control drivers built for quasi-direct-drive legs. Read the docs even if you buy something else, because they explain what a leg actuator needs.
  • ODrive Commercial · open roots · Actuation
    The other standard BLDC driver in hobby robotics, with a gentler learning curve and a large community of working builds.
  • Weekly Robotics Mat Sadowski · free · Newsletter
    Five links a week, no hype, heavy on real engineering. The single cheapest way to stay current once your courses are done.
