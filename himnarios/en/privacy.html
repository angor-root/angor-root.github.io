Privacy policy

This is a translation. If it differs from the Spanish version, the Spanish version prevails.

1. What information is used

The app works without an account, and then everything stays on your phone. If you sign in with Google, we use your name, email and photo. We also use the content you choose to back up or share (lists, notes and marks) and basic data on how features are used.

The PDFs you add are not sent anywhere unless you decide to contribute them.

2. What it is used for

To save and restore your content, share it with your group and improve the app.

3. Device permissions

They are requested only when you turn the feature on:

  • Microphone, for voice notes.
  • Files, to open your scores.
  • Network, to back up, share and update.
  • Nearby devices, to share live and connect the pedal.

4. No selling, no ads

We do not sell information and the app shows no ads.

5. Sign-ups from this website

If you download the app from this website or fill in the iOS form, the name and email you enter (and your answers) are stored only to tell you about news and to reply to you. You can ask for them to be deleted by writing to calebchurata@gmail.com.

6. Deleting your account

To delete your account and data, write to calebchurata@gmail.com from the email of your account.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
